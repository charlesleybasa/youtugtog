## Description

Tugtog is a focused queue and playlist companion for music videos available through YouTube.

Search the music category, add videos to a local queue, reorder what plays next, and save queue snapshots as playlists on your iPhone. Playback uses YouTube's official embedded player and remains visible while it is active.

Features:

- Search for embeddable music videos
- Paste a supported YouTube video link
- Reorder and filter the active queue
- Shuffle and repeat queue playback
- Save, append, replace, and delete local playlists
- Use System, Light, or Dark appearance
- See Up Next from Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
- Keep all queues and playlists on your device

Tugtog does not download videos or extract audio. Playback pauses when the app is covered, backgrounded, or the iPhone is locked.
