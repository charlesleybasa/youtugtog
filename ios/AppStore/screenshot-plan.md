# App Store Screenshot Plan

Capture the current App Store Connect-required iPhone sizes from a release build with representative, policy-compliant data. Do not composite YouTube artwork outside the app UI or imply background audio.

1. **Build your queue** — visible official player, track details, and several queue rows.
2. **Find music quickly** — native Search sheet with loading-complete results and Add actions.
3. **Shape what plays next** — queue reorder and swipe-action affordances.
4. **Save favorite lineups** — local Library showing named playlist snapshots.
5. **Your look, your queue** — polished Dark appearance and Tugtog gradient disc.
6. **Up Next at a glance** — Home Screen small and medium widgets, with playback stopped.

For each shot:

- Use only content available and embeddable at capture time.
- Keep YouTube attribution and official player chrome visible.
- Avoid copyrighted lyrics, downloaded content claims, and lock-screen playback claims.
- Verify legibility at the final uploaded resolution.
- Capture light and dark variants for internal review even if only one set ships.
