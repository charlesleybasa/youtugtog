# App Store Age Rating Working Sheet

Use these answers for the planned v1 binary, then verify them against the current App Store Connect questionnaire immediately before submission. Apple may change the wording or available frequency choices.

## App-provided content and features

- **Advertising:** None in Tugtog itself. The official YouTube player may display YouTube-served advertising as part of third-party video playback.
- **User-generated content:** Yes. Tugtog searches and plays videos hosted by YouTube; it does not host uploads or social interactions.
- **Unrestricted web access:** No. Users cannot browse arbitrary websites inside Tugtog. External YouTube and legal links open using system behavior.
- **Messaging or chat:** None.
- **Contests:** None.
- **Gambling or simulated gambling:** None.
- **Loot boxes:** None.
- **In-app purchases:** None.

## Content descriptors

Tugtog does not create or curate the underlying YouTube videos. Search is limited to the Music category with Moderate SafeSearch, live content is excluded, and only embeddable results are returned. For violence, sexual content, profanity, drugs, alcohol, tobacco, horror, medical information, and mature themes, select the lowest non-zero frequency App Store Connect requires for third-party music-video content unless Apple permits **None** for filtered external catalogs.

## Controls and disclosures

- No account or age-verification system is included in v1.
- No parental gate is included in v1.
- YouTube's own controls, branding, advertising, and outbound links remain intact.
- The final rating must reflect real production search results and any territories where Tugtog is available.
